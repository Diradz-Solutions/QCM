The characters

Max

A curious young explorer with his backpack. Max loves puzzles and learns by asking questions.

Robo-Bit

A silvery robot with blinking lights who thinks in bits, 0s and 1s, like the computers we use every day.

Quinny-Qubit

A magical robot guide in a sparkly wizard hat who floats, twirls, sparkles and thinks in qubits.

Together, the three friends show how classical and quantum computers can work as a team.

The mission

One Million Minds. Infinite Possibilities.
