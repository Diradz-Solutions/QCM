Meet the characters

Three friends, one big adventure

Max

A curious young explorer with his backpack. Max loves puzzles and learns by asking questions.

Robo-Bit

A silvery robot with blinking lights who thinks in bits, 0s and 1s, like the computers we use every day.

Quinny-Qubit

A magical robot guide in a sparkly wizard hat who floats, twirls, sparkles and thinks in qubits.

Together, the three friends show how classical and quantum computers can work as a team.

The Q‑urious Club

A safe online club where young explorers continue the adventure with a grown-up: short videos, quizzes, badges and a certificate.

  1. Read the book

    Start the adventure with Max, Robo-Bit and Quinny-Qubit.

  2. Watch

    Short concept videos.

  3. Quiz

    Playful quizzes to check what you learned.

  4. Earn badges

    Collect badges and a certificate.

  5. Ask

    Send a Quantum Curious Question.

Club sign-ups will be made by a parent or teacher, never by children on their own.
